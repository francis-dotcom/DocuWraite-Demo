It is important for Mary Bet have access to her
personal hearing device and dentures while
out in the community. Mary Bet does not like
walking down the stairs. It is important for
staff to be close by to ensure she remains
safe and follow her Ambulation Staff
Instructions. It is important for Mary Bet to
have access to a wheelchair when she will
be ambulating long distances in the
community. It is important for Mary Bet for
staff to ensure her PKU diet is being followed
as recommended.

and the intermittent wrap around services.

places of her choice and then able to return
home to take a nap, take care of her hygiene
needs, or work on leisure activities. Mary Bet
enjoys going into the community but she
sometimes becomes tired, wants to return
home, and rest..

staff to inform her of upcoming
events/activities she might enjoy. Mary Bet
will decide which activity/event she wants to
do once she has been given her options. It is
also important that Mary Bet is not left in a
vehicle, in the community or in her home.

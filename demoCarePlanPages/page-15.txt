Mary Bet verbally communicates her wants

When Mary Bet is ill, she will appear to be
sad. It is important for staff to pay attention
to her body language. She will also

Mary Bet may have a blank stare or slurred
speech at which time staff should check to
make sure she has taken her medication and
followed her PKU diet. When Mary Bet is in
pain she will tell staff where she is hurting
although she may continue to express pain
even after receiving medical treatment and
this may be due to attention seeking.

When Mary Bet is happy she is smiling,
talking and laughing. When upset Mary Bet
will become quiet and it may become more
difficult to understand what she is saying. It
is important that staff talk with her to help her
work through whatever has upset her.
